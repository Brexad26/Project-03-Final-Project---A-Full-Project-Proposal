# Project Name: Mural for High School Band Hall

## Project Purpose
The high school band program would like to enlist the services of a muralist to design and paint a 5 foot by 10 foot mural in the band hall. The mural will inspire creativity, teamwork, and pride for students in the band. Currently the wall is blank, a visual art piece related to the music department will honor past traditions and inspire future musicians.

## Description/Deliverables
 * Create an inspirational mural for the band hall. 
 * Use right side of the wall for mural
 * Design 3 pieces to present
 * Use acrylic or acrylic latex paint, necessary brushes & materials
 * Get student involvement by doing the following
   * Voting on design choices
   * Helping paint the mural
   * Sharing on socials
 * Reveal completed mural/celebrate
   * First reveal - Administration, Band directors, & current students
   * Second reveal - Student body and parents

## Constraints & Assumptions
 **Time:** Estimated completion is 3-6 weeks
  | Phase   | Duration |
  | ------------- |:-------------:|
  | Design  | 1-3 Weeks    |
  | Wall Prep  | 1-3 days     |
  | Painting   | 1-4 days     |
  | Sealing & Cleanup  | 1 day     |

 **Budget:** Estimated $1000.00 total. Payment to muralist is $500. Paint and supplies will total $500 or less.

 **Quality:** We will have an enhanced entryway to the band hall. Same learning environment but with a new visual inspiration.

## Desired Results
 * Band directors and students will be inspired by their program and continue to foster their craft
 * Students will have a sense of accountability for their part in creating the mural
 * Students will have a sense of pride and teamwork for the band they are apart of
 * The school will have a reminder of their traditions and hope for the future
 
 ## Exceptions/Out of Scope
 Mural is strictly for the high school band hall. Murals will exclude any other walls. New requests for other walls will require another scope statement.
