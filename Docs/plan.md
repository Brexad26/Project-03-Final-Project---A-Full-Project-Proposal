## TAME Analysis
 For the highest risk factor, *Adminstration not approving location*, we will **"M"** mitigate this risk by gaining admin approval prior to the design process. 
 For the next highest risk *Non finalization of design*, we will **T** transfer the risk to a third party. Meaning, we will contract out a muralist for best possible design creation and **M** mitigate this risk by having the student body vote on the top design prior to wall prep.

| Risk  | Score | Strategy | Who |
| ------------- |:-------------:|:-------------:|:-------------:|
| Administration not approving location | 16 | Mitigate: gain prior approval | PM - Brenda Wise |
| Non finalization of design | 12 | Transfer: Have muralist create predetermined designs. Mitigate: Students will vote on final design | Muralist & PM-Brenda Wise|
