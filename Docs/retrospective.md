# Project Retrospective

## Who was interviewed:
* Muralist
* School VP (Administration)
* Band Directors
* Students

## What was done well?
>Stayed within the budget!

>Preplanning went very well - Specifically, getting administration to sign off on mural location

>Getting designs done on time as it was critical in getting the mural started

>Getting the students involved - Students voted for the winning design and help create a volunteer schedule.

## What didnt go well?
>Did not estimate how much paint was needed. While we did not go over budget, there was a lot of excess.

>Did not consider band competition schedules, which did impact the completion time slightly

## What can we improve on?
>We can improve on the design process. It seems important to the students to contribute to the design or elements of the mural.

>We can improve on communicating and confirming working hours with band directors. This is referencing the competition schedules as there were times the hall was unavailable due to everyone being at a competition.
