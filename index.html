Band Hall Mural Project

Lions Mural

This is the website to display information about the Lion's Mural project.

Once the mural is complete, it will be displayed in the band hall for all to see. The mural will showcase the spirit and pride of our school. Additionally, it will serve as a source of inspiration for students and staff alike.

This website will also provide updates on the progress of the mural and information about the artists involved.
